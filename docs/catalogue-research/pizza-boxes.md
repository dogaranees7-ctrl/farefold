# FareFold catalogue design research — pizza boxes

Status: reference research only; no customer-facing product claims or product photography.

## Objective

Build an original FareFold pizza-box range by studying real, manufactured packaging examples. Use references to understand structural ideas and customer experience, then develop FareFold-specific dielines, artwork and physical samples. Do not reuse third-party logos, brand artwork, product photographs or distinctive trade dress without written permission.

## Reference directions

### 1. Multi-level meal and promotion packaging

**Reference:** Smurfit Westrock's Papa Johns customer story  
https://www.us.smurfitwestrock.com/innovation/customer-stories/papa-johns

The published example describes a three-tiered unit designed to carry multiple pizzas and sides. Its sleeve can also become an interactive game surface.

**FareFold exploration:** A family-meal carrier with separate levels or compartments, plus an optional detachable activity panel for promotions. Prototype carrying strength, stacking, assembly time and food separation before offering it.

### 2. Dual-wing opening and ventilation

**Reference:** Red Dot Design Award — The Next-Gen Gull-Wing Corrugated Pizza Box  
https://www.red-dot.org/project/the-next-gen-gull-wing-corrugated-pizza-box-90135

The award entry describes a dual-wing lid opening, simplified folding, flat pre-folded storage and integrated ventilation.

**FareFold exploration:** Test a quick-open lid with simple locking geometry and ventilation positioned for the intended pizza and delivery journey. Validate grease resistance, heat retention, stack strength and assembly on a physical sample. Do not repeat the award entry's material-saving percentage as a FareFold claim.

### 3. Box that converts into a serving tray

**Reference:** European Product Design Award — Sustainable pizza packaging (Bizongo / Ovenstory)  
https://www.productdesignaward.eu/winners/epda/2022/10717/

The award entry describes a delivery-focused box designed to reduce movement of the pizza during transit and convert into a serving tray.

**FareFold exploration:** Develop a fold-out or tear-away serving surface that can be assembled without tools. Test whether the structure stays secure when carried at different angles and whether the conversion is intuitive.

### 4. Raised, ventilated pizza base

**Reference:** KEEPizza  
https://www.keepizza.it/

The manufacturer's product page shows a raised internal support structure intended to let air circulate beneath the pizza.

**FareFold exploration:** Evaluate a raised support or vented insert only where it can be manufactured efficiently and remains stable with the intended board, pizza size and delivery conditions.

## First prototype shortlist

1. Standard square delivery box with carefully placed ventilation.
2. Quick-open dual-wing lid format.
3. Fold-out serving tray / detachable slice support.
4. Raised-base or insert-assisted ventilation format.
5. Multi-compartment family carrier for pizza plus sides.

## Checks before a design becomes a FareFold offer

- Confirm the exact board, coating, inks and adhesives are appropriate for the intended food-contact use with the relevant supplier documentation and applicable Pakistan requirements.
- Prototype with the actual pizza size, weight, temperature, toppings and delivery bag.
- Test grease migration, condensation, ventilation, lid closure, stack load, drop/tilt handling and assembly time.
- Confirm whether the design is protected by patents, registered designs or other IP in target markets; commission an original adaptation rather than copying protected construction or trade dress.
- Photograph the approved physical FareFold sample before publishing it as a FareFold product.
- Publish only specifications and performance claims demonstrated by FareFold's own sample and production process.
